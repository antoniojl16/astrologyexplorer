    ABOUT

    Orbital Study

    A workspace for studying birth charts through four lenses — Astrology, Human Design, Gene Keys and Astrocartography — on their own, over time, between two people, across life cycles and against the events of a life.

    LIFE EVENTS

    Record the moments, periods and places of a life — alone or shared with other charts in the workspace — and annotate Birth and cycle moments with places, tags and notes. Study any of them in the Cycle Explorer (with a summary of what was active in each system), or the sky right now as transits to a chart, see two lives side by side in the Pair Explorer, and browse them by tag to find the transits that recur across moments with the same theme.

    YOUR DATA

    Charts and life events are stored only in this browser, on this device; nothing is sent to a server, and shared links never include life events. Use Export workspace to keep a backup or move your charts and events elsewhere, or select charts in the library to export only those. Importing a file asks what to do with charts already here: skip, replace or copy them. Import workspace also reads charts from a CSV (Name, Date, Time, Timezone, City, Country), as other Human Design apps export them.

    ACCURACY

    Positions come from Astronomy Engine. Checked against the Swiss Ephemeris, the Sun, Moon and planets agree to within about 20 seconds of arc (0.005°), the lunar nodes and mean Lilith to within half a minute, and cycle dates to within hours. Chiron is simulated from NASA JPL orbit data and agrees with JPL to within a few seconds of arc from 1800 to 2200.

    Birth times are converted with the IANA time zone database, daylight saving included; for births before 1970 it adds the historical time zones browsers leave out, and before standard time was adopted it uses the birthplace’s own local mean time.
